# Zentra Cloud: Pricing and Plans

Zentra Cloud offers four plans: Free, Pro, Team and Enterprise. All prices are in US dollars and exclude local taxes.

## Free plan

The Free plan costs $0 per month. It includes 1 project, 5 GB of storage and 1,000 API calls per day. Support is community-only through the public forum.

## Pro plan

The Pro plan costs $29 per month per user. It includes 20 projects, 200 GB of storage and 50,000 API calls per day. Pro customers get email support with a 24-hour response time.

## Team plan

The Team plan costs $99 per month per user and requires a minimum of 5 users. It includes unlimited projects, 2 TB of storage and 500,000 API calls per day. Team customers get priority support with a 4-hour response time and single sign-on through SAML.

## Enterprise plan

Enterprise pricing is custom and negotiated with the sales team. Enterprise customers receive a dedicated support manager, a 99.99% uptime SLA and custom data residency options. The standard plans (Free, Pro and Team) have a 99.9% uptime target but no financial SLA.

## Billing

Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month. Annual billing gives a 20% discount compared with monthly billing. Customers can pay by credit card, and wire transfer is accepted only for annual invoices above $5,000.

## Refunds and cancellation

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time, and refunds are given only within 14 days of the first payment. Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of purchase.

## Overage charges

Extra storage costs $0.10 per GB per month. Extra API calls beyond the daily limit cost $0.50 per 1,000 calls.

## Discounts

Students get a 50% discount on the Pro plan with a verified .edu email address. Non-profit organisations receive 30% off the Team plan.

## Free trials

The Pro and Team plans include a 14-day free trial that does not require a credit card. At the end of the trial the workspace moves to the Free plan unless a payment method is added.

## Upgrading and downgrading

Upgrades take effect immediately and the price difference is prorated for the rest of the billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. If a downgrade would put the workspace above the storage limit of the new plan, the workspace becomes read-only until the data is reduced.

## Adding and removing seats

Seats can be added at any time and are prorated. Administrators can remove seats at any time, but credit for removed seats is applied to the next invoice rather than refunded.

## Taxes

VAT and GST are calculated at checkout based on the billing address. Customers with a valid tax ID can enter it in the billing settings to have the tax removed where the law allows it.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, Zentra retries the charge 3 times over 7 days. If all retries fail, the workspace is suspended and becomes read-only. Data is kept for 60 days after suspension before it is deleted.

## Invoice currencies

Invoices are issued in US dollars by default. Annual plans can also be invoiced in euros, British pounds or Indian rupees.
