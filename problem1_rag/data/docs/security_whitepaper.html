Zentra Cloud Security Whitepaper

This document describes how Zentra Cloud protects customer data.

Encryption

All customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. TLS 1.2 is the minimum version accepted for older clients.

Enterprise customers can bring their own customer-managed keys. Encryption keys are rotated automatically every 365 days.

Certifications

  • Zentra Cloud holds a SOC 2 Type II report that is audited every year.
  • Zentra Cloud is certified against ISO 27001.
  • A HIPAA business associate agreement is available for Enterprise customers.

Data retention and backups

Deleted data is purged from active systems within 30 days. Backups are retained for 35 days and are replicated across 3 availability zones. Team customers get point-in-time recovery for up to 30 days.

Incident response

Customers are notified within 72 hours of a confirmed data breach. Live service status is published on the public status page.

Access control

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all Zentra staff. Production access requires just-in-time approval that expires after 4 hours.

Vulnerability disclosure

Zentra runs a bug bounty programme. Rewards range from $100 to $10,000 depending on severity. Reports should be sent to the security team, who respond within 3 business days.

Penetration testing

An independent third party performs penetration tests twice a year.

Network security

Production systems run inside private networks, and no database accepts connections from the public internet. A web application firewall and DDoS protection sit in front of every public endpoint.

Audit logs

Audit logs are kept for 30 days on the Pro plan, 90 days on the Team plan and 400 days on the Enterprise plan.

Data residency

Customer data can be stored in the United States, the European Union or Singapore. Data stored in the European Union stays in the Frankfurt and Dublin regions.

Subprocessors

Zentra publishes a list of subprocessors and gives customers 30 days of notice before adding a new one.

Employee screening and training

All employees pass a background check before they start. Every employee completes security awareness training once a year.

Disaster recovery

Enterprise customers have a recovery point objective of 15 minutes and a recovery time objective of 4 hours. The disaster recovery plan is tested twice a year.
